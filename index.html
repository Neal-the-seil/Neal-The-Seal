About Neil

Neil is a wild elephant seal who was born in Tasmania and comes back every mating season and shedding season. He is 5 years old and is famous for causing all manner of problems.

Traffic cone war

Neil loves traffic cones. He turns them from a perfect cone to a pancake in seconds. They come in handy when we need to move him, like when he is on the road.

How we can protect Neil

Some ways you can help protect Neil, which Tasmania wildlife and parks have said, are: keep a good distance from Neil, around 50 meters. You can look and film Neil, but do not touch him because he will defend himself.
