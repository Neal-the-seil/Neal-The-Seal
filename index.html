About Neil

Neil is a wild seal who visits Tasmania every mating and shedding season. He is approximately 5 years old and is famous locally for causing all manner of problems — from breaking fences to upsetting traffic. He is wild; please enjoy him from a safe distance.

Traffic cone war

Neil loves traffic cones — he can flatten a perfect cone into a pancake in seconds. Cones sometimes help us guide him off roads, but they also end up as his toys. Please keep a safe distance if you see him around traffic.

How we can protect Neil

Some recommended ways to protect Neil (advice from Tasmania Parks & Wildlife): keep at least 50 metres distance, observe and film from afar but do not approach or feed him. If Neil is on the road, contact local wildlife authorities for guidance.
